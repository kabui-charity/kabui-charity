## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📍 Geospatial Hotspot Analysis — Govern
Identified activity hotspots across City of Tshwane Region 1 using H3 hexagonal spatial indexing and Getis-Ord Gi\* statistics,  mapping app-usage hotspots to reveal high-engagement zones and guide targeted marketing and expansion decisions.

**Stack:** `Python` `GeoPandas` `Folium` `H3`
**Impact:** Surfaced statistically significant clusters of app usage, giving the team a data-driven view of where engagement was concentrated for marketing targeting.

---

### 📊 Insurance Data — Exploratory & Statistical Analysis
Cleaned and analyzed an insurance dataset to surface risk and pricing patterns for business decision-making.

**Stack:** `Python` `Pandas` `Matplotlib`

---

### 💳 Credit Score Prediction for the Unbanked
Built an ML pipeline using alternative data to assess creditworthiness for individuals without traditional credit histories.

**Stack:** `scikit-learn` `Feature Engineering` `Model Evaluation`
**Impact:** Demonstrated that alternative data sources can meaningfully predict creditworthiness for individuals lacking traditional credit history extending assessment beyond conventional credit scoring.
